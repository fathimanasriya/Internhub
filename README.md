# Student Internship Record System - SIRS

## Introduction

SIRS - Student Internship Record System is a web-based platform designed to streamline and manage the internship process for students and HR/recruiters. The system allows students to maintain and track their internship records, discover suitable internship opportunities, and improve their resumes based on their career goals.

For HR and recruiters, the platform provides an organized way to manage internship opportunities, view student profiles, and identify students whose skills and qualifications align with the requirements of available internships.

The system also incorporates AI-powered features and ATS-style resume analysis to improve the internship matching and resume-building experience.

## Features

### 1.Student

- Create and manage a student profile
- Record and track internship details
- Upload and analyze resumes
- Specify preferred job roles
- Find internships that match their skills and preferences
- Identify missing skills for specific internship roles
- Receive AI-powered resume improvement suggestions
- Interact with an AI chatbot for resume and career-related assistance
- Track internship applications and their status

### 2.HR

- Create and manage internship opportunities
- Specify required skills and qualifications
- View student profiles
- Review student resumes
- Find students matching internship requirements
- Manage internship applications
- Track candidates and application status

## AI Implementions

### AI Internship Matching

The system analyzes a student's resume, skills, and preferred job role and compares them with available internship requirements to identify relevant opportunities.
The AI can consider:

- Technical skills
- Educational background
- Projects and experience
- Preferred job role
- Internship requirements

### AI Resume Assistant

An AI-powered chatbot helps students improve their resumes by answering questions such as:

- What is missing from my resume?
- How can I improve my project description?
- What skills should I learn for a particular role?
- How can I make my resume more relevant to a job?
- How should I describe my internship experience?
